Has this password been leaked?

Checks against Have I Been Pwned's database of breached passwords. Your password never leaves this page.

How: your browser hashes the password with SHA-1 and sends only the first 5 of 40 hash characters to the range API. It gets back every leaked hash starting with those 5 and looks for yours locally. Responses are padded so their size gives nothing away.
